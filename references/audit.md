# Audit

Use independent judges for Source Audit, Universe Audit, Premarket Red Team and Final Review Audit. Researcher self-assessment is not proof.

Hard failures include material source/image gaps, unresolved count mismatches, future-data contamination, StockBase permission violations, early recall pruning, unsupported role claims, known universe omissions, and rule promotion without replay.

Final completion requires frozen integrity plus all Must-Pass gates.
