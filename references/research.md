# Research: recall first

Research must know substantially more than execution selects.

Build an All-News Event Ledger before prioritization. Preserve weak, negative, disproved, rumor, calendar and no-trade events so omission audits are possible.

IndustryResearch is the many-to-many research substrate: one event can map to many nodes, and one stock can legitimately appear under multiple events/nodes. Do not destroy those mappings merely to deduplicate the execution universe.

Universe is the stock-level active main-board view derived from that research substrate. Every main-board-eligible mapped ticker must be accounted for exactly one of two ways: included in active Universe, or explicitly excluded with a durable reason. Every researched industry node must have at least one stock mapping or an explicit node-level exclusion reason. `all_event_nodes_exhausted=true` is a claim that must reconcile mechanically with these records, not a substitute for them.

U0 is exactly the active Universe ticker set. U1 is the Potential Limit-Up Recall pool and must remain a subset of U0. Leader-First applies only after U1 and may not prune research recall. A final U3 candidate cannot appear from outside U1.

Use at least two materially different research sweeps. Stop only with an auditable reason. Record counterevidence and unresolved gaps.
