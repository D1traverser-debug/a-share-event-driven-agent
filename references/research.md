# Research: recall first

Research must know substantially more than execution selects.

Build an All-News Event Ledger before prioritization. Preserve weak, negative, disproved, rumor, calendar and no-trade events so omission audits are possible.

U0 is all event/industry/company mappings. U1 is the Potential Limit-Up Recall pool. Leader-First applies only after U1 and may not prune research recall.

Use at least two materially different research sweeps. Stop only with an auditable reason. Record counterevidence and unresolved gaps.
