# Learning governance

Daily review may generate hypotheses; it may not rewrite durable rules directly.

A durable rule proposal must name one canonical owner, hypothesis, detection rule, prevention rule, applicable regimes, counterexamples and replay cases.

Promotion requires replay over at least five frozen cases, at least two regimes, positive and negative samples, zero future-information contamination and no material regression. If evidence is insufficient, HOLD/N_A is correct.

Rule promotion should be implemented through a tested GitHub change, not by silently mutating runtime text/state.
