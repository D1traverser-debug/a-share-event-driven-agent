# Short-term market structure

Board count is a fact, not a role proof.

Maintain HeightMap plus evidence-backed hypotheses for SpaceLeader, ThemeLeader, NewThemePioneer, CardLeader, TurnoverLeader, TrendLeader, CapacityLeader, SupplementLeader, Anchor and Follower.

A role hypothesis requires multiple evidence dimensions: leadership/initiative, theme breadth, same-height competition, capacity-anchor feedback, prior identity, event binding, turnover/board quality when available, and disconfirming evidence.

Also maintain same-height PK, Role Vacancy, Continuation, Rotation, High-Low, Capacity Rotation, Transmission next-leg, and failure scenarios.
