# Deep industry parity

Recommendation depth may not be shallower than review depth. Core events require at least L0-L5 unless the analyst documents why a shorter chain is economically complete.

For each core event map, as relevant:
- product/BOM;
- upstream materials;
- equipment/consumables;
- process/technology route;
- customers and suppliers;
- orders and business stage (R&D/sample/certification/small batch/mass production/mature);
- capacity/utilization/inventory;
- price/cost/pass-through;
- revenue/profit/cash-flow transmission;
- beneficiaries and harmed nodes.

Recall stocks node-by-node before exclusion. Business directness is a variable, not an automatic short-term winner.
