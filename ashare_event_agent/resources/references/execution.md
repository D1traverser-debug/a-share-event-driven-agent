# Execution planning

Execution is narrow after recall is broad.

Default U3 has at most four roles:
1. Primary Leader
2. Backup Leader
3. NewTheme Pioneer
4. Capacity Anchor

Every U3 ticker must descend from the frozen U1 recall pool. Execution ranking may narrow U1 but may not introduce a stock that research/recall never admitted. Candidate tickers and role slots are unique.

## Opportunity first, risk second

Event/industry alpha and market-structure leadership are **opportunity** views. They may compete and be adjudicated into a preliminary opportunity slate.

Risk/buyability is not a third bullish vote. Every frozen U1 candidate must receive an explicit risk assessment before final U3 adjudication:

- `PASS` — no hard veto found under the observed evidence;
- `DEGRADE` — candidate remains eligible but evidence/risk quality is weaker and must be surfaced;
- `BLOCK` — hard risk or non-executability veto; the ticker may not enter final U3.

Each risk assessment requires evidence refs. `BLOCK` requires explicit hard-veto reasons. Missing preferred 同花顺/Level-2 evidence cannot support FULL execution assurance; use DEGRADED assurance or NO TRADE as appropriate.

Each non-empty final candidate requires strategy type, trigger, invalidation, why-this and **why-not-peers**. `why_not_peers` is mandatory because selection quality is comparative: stating why a stock is good is not enough to establish why it beat the other recalled candidates.

NO TRADE is a first-class legal result. An empty candidate list is valid only when no-trade conditions and the backup chain remain explicit; the system must not fill four slots merely because four slots exist.

LeaderScore and Buyability are separate. A stock can be the true space leader and still be a poor or impossible new-entry trade.
